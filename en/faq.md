# FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

## Login

### I forgot my password

Contact your administrator to request a password reset.

### My account is locked

Your account is locked after 5 or more failed password attempts.
Contact your administrator to request account unlock.

## Schedule Management

### Can I see other people's schedules?

You can view schedules of members in the same department on the calendar.
Access to other departments' schedules may be restricted based on permissions.

### I can't edit a schedule

You can only edit schedules you created.
Already approved vacation schedules must be canceled and re-applied.

## Vacation Management

### My remaining vacation days seem incorrect

Contact your administrator to verify vacation accrual/usage history.

### How do I apply for a half-day?

Select **Half-day** in **Vacation Type** when applying for vacation.
You can select morning or afternoon.

### I want to cancel my vacation request

- **Pending**: Can cancel in Electronic Approval
- **Approved**: Request cancellation from administrator

## Electronic Approval

### Can I change the approval line?

The default approval line is automatically set according to the organization chart.
Contact your administrator if changes are needed.

### My approval is delayed

Directly contact the approver.
Check if a proxy approver is set when the approver is absent.

## Others

### Can I use it on mobile?

Yes, POREST is a responsive web that works on mobile browsers.

### Are languages other than Korean supported?

Currently Korean and English are supported.
You can change the language in profile settings.
