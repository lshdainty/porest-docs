# Schedule Management

View and manage work and vacation schedules on a calendar.

## Calendar View

### Monthly View

View a month's schedule at a glance.

- Vacations, business trips, training, etc. are color-coded
- Click a date to view detailed schedules for that day

### Weekly View

View detailed schedules on a weekly basis.

### Daily View

View hourly schedules for a day.

## Register Schedule

### Personal Schedule

1. **Click a date** on the calendar
2. Click **Add Schedule** button
3. Enter schedule information
   - Title
   - Start date / End date
   - Schedule type (Vacation, Business trip, Training, etc.)
   - Notes (optional)
4. Click **Save** button

### Common Schedules

Quickly register common schedules like military training, education, etc.

::: tip Quick Registration
Save frequently used schedule types as templates for quick registration.
:::

## Schedule Filtering

### Department Filter

Filter to view only a specific department's schedules.

1. Click **Filter** icon at the top of the calendar
2. Select desired department
3. Click **Apply** button

### Individual Filter

Filter to view only a specific member's schedules.

## Edit/Delete Schedule

### Edit

1. **Click the schedule** to edit on the calendar
2. Click **Edit** button
3. Modify information and **Save**

### Delete

1. **Click the schedule** to delete on the calendar
2. Click **Delete** button
3. Confirm deletion

::: warning Caution
Deleted schedules cannot be recovered.
:::
