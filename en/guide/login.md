# Login

## How to Login

When you access POREST, the login screen will be displayed.

### Standard Login

1. Enter your issued ID in the **ID** field
2. Enter your password in the **Password** field
3. Click the **Login** button

::: tip Don't know your ID?
Contact your administrator to confirm your ID.
:::

### Social Login

OAuth2-based social login is supported.

- Login with Google account

## Change Password

It is recommended to change your password on first login or for security purposes.

1. After login, click the **Profile** icon in the upper right
2. Select **Change Password** menu
3. Enter current password and new password
4. Click **Save** button

::: warning Password Rules
- Minimum 8 characters
- Combination of letters, numbers, and special characters recommended
:::

## Logout

1. Click the **Profile** icon in the upper right
2. Select **Logout** menu
